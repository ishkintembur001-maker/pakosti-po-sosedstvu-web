# Fine Grained Wood

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/fine_grained_wood
Author: Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven.
License: CC0 1.0 Universal. https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Poly Haven license statement: https://polyhaven.com/license

Offline derivatives: 4K JPEG maps resized to 1024 with Lanczos filtering; tangent normals renormalized. Original and derived SHA-256 hashes and exact source URLs are in materials.json. No network access is needed to rebuild the project. The game applies its own palette tint and roughness adjustment.
